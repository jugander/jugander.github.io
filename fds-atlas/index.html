Methodology & sources

01

Scope and time window

The network uses the official Yale FDS Members roster as of October 7, 2026. Publications are included when a source dates them from January 1, 2023 through October 7, 2026, inclusive. Nodes show members who appear on at least one confirmed shared work.

02

Publication evidence

Records came from Crossref, arXiv, and Europe PMC. Candidate coauthors were screened against Yale profiles and author identifiers. Distinct bibliographic versions were grouped into intellectual works using identifiers, matching titles, and documented version links.

03

Edges and departments

Each edge joins two confirmed FDS coauthors. Edge weight is the number of distinct shared works, counted once per work even when several database records or versions exist. The layout finds connected components before force simulation, seeds the largest near the center, and places smaller components to its left or right. Repulsion acts within each component, while a soft vertical guard keeps leaf nodes near the main component; the main component is biased wider than tall. Faculty sharing a primary department start near one another, with a light departmental pull during relaxation. A pair is within-department when its members share at least one primary department; joint primary appointments count in both departments. A paper is cross-department if it contains at least one cross-department FDS pair. School groups follow Yale's Medicine, FAS, Engineering, Public Health, and Management directories. MBB is listed by both FAS and Medicine; for this single-choice legend it appears under Medicine.

04

What the controls do

Within-department network edges remain visible in gray and interactive. School and department checkboxes filter only network nodes and edges; a partly selected school shows a mixed-state checkbox. Click blank network space to clear a pinned selection. The pair-table and bibliography within-department controls act independently and default to on: the former hides within-department pairs, while the latter hides papers whose FDS coauthor pairs are all within-department. The bibliography sorts by the latest indexed version date, newest first; bold names identify confirmed FDS faculty. When the selected source author list omits a confirmed member, that name is appended with an explanatory tooltip.

This is a high-confidence, best-effort indexed dataset, not a complete census of every faculty CV. Sources may omit books and working papers, lag new publications, or split author identities.
